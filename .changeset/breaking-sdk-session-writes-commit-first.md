breaking(sdk): Session writes now resolve only after they are saved, and nothing reads or observes an entry before its save commits.

`SessionManager` write methods (`appendMessage`, `appendThinkingLevelChange`, `appendFastModeChange`, `appendModelChange`, `appendPlanningState`, `appendCompaction`, `appendCustomEntry`, `appendSessionInfo`, `appendCustomMessageEntry`, `appendLabelChange`, `appendSubagentSpawn`, `branch`, `resetLeaf`, `branchWithSummary`, `newSession`, `recordStartingGitContext`, `recordPrReviewBinding`, `reserveClientInput`, `markClientInputQueued`, `rollbackClientInput`, `transitionClientInput`) are async and resolve after the write commits; until then its entries are not in `getEntries()`, `getBranch()`, or `buildSessionContext()`. `AgentSession.updatePlan`, `updatePlanProgress`, `requestReplan`, `submitPlan`, `changePlan`, `discardPlan`, `setFastModeEnabled`, `setSessionName`, and `recordBashResult` are async too, and the extension API's `volt.appendEntry`, `volt.setLabel`, and `volt.setSessionName` return a `Promise<void>`. To migrate, await each write before reading what it wrote. `SessionManager.flush()`, `SessionManager.materialize()`, `SessionManager.getIndexedOrdinal()`, and `AgentSession.flushPlanningState()` are removed: await the writes instead, and use `closePersistence()` to wait for every write before closing. `SessionManager.getCommittedOrdinal()` is renamed `getOrdinal()`. A write whose save is rolled back rejects with `SessionAtomicAppendError` and leaves the session unchanged and writable.
