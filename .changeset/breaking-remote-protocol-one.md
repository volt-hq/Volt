breaking(remote): Paired devices now speak Volt protocol 1 over Iroh ALPN `volt/1`: a phone subscribes to its conversation's log, receives entries and a live lane on the remote profile, sends intents, and runs queries, whether the daemon or a desktop TUI serves the conversation. ([#585](https://github.com/volt-hq/Volt/issues/585))

The remote profile carries transcript views only, redacts workspace and worktree paths and provider signatures from every frame at one send, bounds snapshots to the last 200 entries (older ones through `history`) and resumes to 1,000 entries (a snapshot replaces a longer gap), caps each connection at 16 subscriptions and a refilling budget of 16 snapshot, replay, and history reads, resets a device that falls more than 64 MiB behind instead of slowing the conversation, and ends a stream whose frame exceeds 4 MiB with `fatal{frame_too_large}`. Every frame rechecks the device's grant: a stream whose grant or workspace authorization changed ends with `fatal{revoked}`, and revoking a device or changing its access closes its connections at once. A phone's session change answers `accepted{conversation}` and ends its stream with `ended{moved}`; a TUI handoff ends it with `ended{closed}`. Workspace discovery and management streams serve their purpose's queries and intents. Approvals reach only devices granted `host.manage.v1`, and extension form patterns that could backtrack without bound are refused. MCP calls report progress as live tool items, and `changed` now also announces `sessions`, `intents`, `extensions`, and `host` (keep-awake, web search key, and the shared theme, now the `host_status` query's `theme`). Completion notifications are push only. The `settings` query names the active settings profile that the compaction intents expect, and a branch-fenced intent is rejected `stale` when the branch switches while it runs.

Migration: update paired apps to protocol 1 (see [docs/iroh-remote-protocol.md](https://github.com/volt-hq/Volt/blob/main/packages/coding-agent/docs/iroh-remote-protocol.md)); a device on `volt-rpc/0` can no longer connect. Restart the daemon after upgrading: the daemon control protocol is now version 3, and `relay_rpc` carries intent and query frames. In the SDK, serve a paired device's stream with `serveIrohRemoteConnection` instead of `runIrohRemoteRpcMode`; the Iroh command filter, outbound filter, and transcript text exports (`createIrohRemoteFilteredRpcTransport`, `createIrohRemoteOutboundFilteredRpcTransport`, `sanitizeIrohRemoteOutbound`, `getIrohRemoteRpcCommandCapabilities`, the `handleIrohRemote*RpcCommand` handlers, the `IROH_REMOTE_*_RPC_TYPE` constants, and their types) and `ConversationTranscriptCommittedEvent` are removed; `uploadIrohRemoteDeviceLog` uploads device logs, and `attachCompletionNotifications` pushes a device's completion notifications.
