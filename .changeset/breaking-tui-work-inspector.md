breaking(tui): `/work` and Alt+J open one work inspector for jobs, subagents, reviews, host actions, and extension work, replacing `/jobs`, `/subagents`, and their keys; a conversation stays open, and a session switch waits, while its work runs. ([#585](https://github.com/volt-hq/Volt/issues/585))

The inspector lists every work item with its state (suspended and awaiting approval included), progress, kind detail, result, and newest output, and offers what each kind allows: Ctrl+K cancels after a confirmation, Ctrl+R resumes suspended work, and Enter opens the conversation the work runs in or produced. A subagent opens read-only, also after it ended or a restart suspended it. The footer shows open work on one line; a finished job's launch card shows its recorded outcome after a restart instead of "Running at capture"; an extension's own notice text renders as Markdown; notices of finished work queued for the next turn show above the editor; and work that delivers no notice says how it ended in a status line.

Migration: use `/work` instead of `/jobs` and `/subagents`. In `keybindings.json`, rename `app.jobs.open` to `app.work.open`, `app.jobs.cancel` to `app.work.cancel`, and `app.jobs.follow` to `app.work.follow`; `app.work.resume` (Ctrl+R) is new, and `app.subagents.open`, `app.subagents.previous`, and `app.subagents.next` are removed. Leaving a conversation (`/new`, `/resume`, `/fork`, and their intents) is refused while its work runs; cancel the work or wait for it. A daemon keeps a detached conversation while work runs or an operation holds it, but no longer for work awaiting approval or suspended work.

Protocol clients: `open_work` on a subagent whose conversation closed answers its conversation, and subscribing to a closed subagent conversation your conversation links answers a snapshot of its log, then `ended{closed}`. Work progress text, step labels, and step keys a host cut no longer show the start of a host path on paired devices, and a work checkpoint or live work value stays within its bound after redaction (its detail, then steps, are dropped). Extensions can no longer send `work_notice` custom messages, which are the host's. SDK callers use `HostedConversation.isActive()` and `waitForIdle()` instead of `session.hasRunningWork` and `session.work.waitForIdle()` for retention, `WorkRegistry.busy()` for running work, and `WorkRecord.checkpoints`; a kind's `maxActive` now counts suspended work, and an item records at most 256 checkpoints.
